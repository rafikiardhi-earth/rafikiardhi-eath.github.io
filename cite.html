How to cite

Citations for the research tools, plausibility checks, soil classification keys, the Soil Colour Reader and the homeowner tools.

If a tool helped your work, please cite the tool and the published methods it implements. The method references are listed with each tool; they credit the people whose science the tool is built on.

All tools are version 1.0. Persistent identifiers (DOIs) will be added here as they are minted; until then, cite the web address.

  • Flynn, T. (2026). South African soil classification key (2018), version 1.0 [software]. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23738161

Please also cite the classification itself: IUSS Working Group WRB (2022), World Reference Base for Soil Resources, 4th edition, International Union of Soil Sciences, Vienna; Santos, H.G. dos et al. (2018), Sistema Brasileiro de Classificação de Solos, 5th edition, Embrapa, Brasília; or Soil Classification Working Group (2018), Soil Classification: A Natural and Anthropogenic System for South Africa, ARC-Institute for Soil, Climate and Water, Pretoria.
