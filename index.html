What we believe

  • Build on what exists. Old surveys and past projects are worth more than people think. We update knowledge instead of replace it.
  • Honest uncertainty. Every answer comes with how far it can be trusted. We propogate uncertainties and make them informative.
  • No AI, no black boxes. Our methods follow the processes behind the environment, so every result can be traced and checked.
  • Science in your own language. Our tools and keys come in the languages people actually speak, not only English. Want to help?
  • Land is shared. Healthy land includes the animals that live with us and the attitude we bring to it.

Homeowners: open science

Practical home and landscape decisions informed by the same scientific principles researchers use, from applying Darcy’s and Jurin’s laws for irrigation design to using calculus to estimate sod quantities for irregular lawns.

Researchers: creative freedom

Let your mind explore the scientific landscape, without compromising scientific rigour. Discover free research tools and checks, creative approaches to difficult environmental problems, and new ways to challenge the possible.
